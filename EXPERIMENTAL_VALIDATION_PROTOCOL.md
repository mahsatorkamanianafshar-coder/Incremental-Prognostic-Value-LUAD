# Experimental validation protocol

Computational candidates must not be described as validated therapies before completing this protocol.

## Minimum study

1. Test at least two LUAD cell lines with distinct molecular backgrounds and one non-malignant lung epithelial line.
2. Use at least six concentrations, vehicle controls, and three independent biological replicates.
3. Estimate IC50 and compare the therapeutic window between malignant and non-malignant cells.
4. Measure viability, apoptosis, migration/invasion, and clonogenic survival.
5. Confirm engagement of the nominated drug target(s) and measure the relevant
   frozen-model/network-priority gene expression or activity using an orthogonal assay.
6. Include positive and negative controls and randomize plate positions.
7. Report blinded analysis, raw measurements, exclusion criteria, and uncertainty intervals.

Candidate status should remain **computational hypothesis** until these experiments and an independent replication are complete.
